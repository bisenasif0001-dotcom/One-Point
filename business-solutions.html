GST & MSME

Documents for GST & MSME

PAN Card of Proprietor or Firm

Mandatory tax ID required for setting up business profiles.

Aadhaar of Owner / Partner

Linked mobile number required to complete Aadhaar OTP e-signature check.

Business Address Proof

Electricity bill, Rent deed, or NOC from the property owner.

Bank Details or Cancelled Cheque

Required to verify and link active bank account details on the portal.

Shop & IEC

Documents for Shop License & Import Export

Owner Identity Proof

Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport of the business owner.

Shop Front Photograph

A clear photo showing the shop banner and entry point (for Shop License).

Address Proof of Business Place

Electricity bill, Rent agreement, or ownership proof of premises.

Company & DSC

Documents for Company Registration & DSC

PAN & Aadhaar Card

Mandatory for all proposed directors or partners.

Passport Size Photograph

Recent color photograph with white background.

Active Email & Mobile Number

Required for DSC video verification and OTP authorization.

Proposed Company Names

1 or 2 unique names for MCA portal check and approval.

Food & TM

Documents for FSSAI License & Trademark

Owner ID & Photo

Aadhaar, PAN Card, and passport photo of applicant.

Business Address Proof

Electricity bill, rent deed, or NOC from the owner.

Logo Image / Brand Name

High-resolution JPEG or PNG logo and user affidavit (for Trademark).

Food Category List

Detailed list of food items manufactured, processed or sold (for FSSAI).

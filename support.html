Quick Answers

Common support questions

Find quick answers below. For anything else, WhatsApp us directly.

How do I pay for online services?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), cash at the center, and bank transfer. For remote requests, send the payment screenshot as confirmation.

Can I visit the center physically?

Yes! Walk in anytime between 9 AM and 8 PM, Monday to Sunday. Our team will assist you directly at the counter.

Are my documents and data safe?

Yes. Documents are used only for the requested service and never shared. We follow all UIDAI guidelines. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I check my application status?

Use our Track Application page with your tracking ID, or WhatsApp us your tracking ID for an instant status update.

What if my application gets rejected?

If the rejection is due to our error, we re-process at no extra charge. For rejections due to wrong documents submitted by you, a re-application charge applies. See Refund Policy.

Do you work on Sundays and holidays?

We are open Monday to Sunday, 9 AM to 8 PM. On major public holidays we may have reduced hours — WhatsApp us to confirm availability.

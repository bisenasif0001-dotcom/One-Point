Who Can Apply for a PAN Card?

Any individual or entity requiring a tax identification number in India is eligible:

Individual Citizens (Adults) Any resident or non-resident Indian citizen aged 18 and above requiring PAN for banking or employment.
Minors & Students (Under 18) Parents or legal guardians can apply on behalf of children for scholarship, bank accounts, or investments.
Firms, Companies & Trusts Proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, private limited companies, and trusts.
NRIs & Foreign Citizens (Form 49AA) Non-resident Indians and foreign passport holders having taxable financial interests in India.

Important PAN Card Information & Advisory

Please review our operating standards, statutory fee transparency, and compliance terms before starting.

Independent Facilitation Desk

One Point Digital Services is a private assisted service provider. We offer application drafting, format validation, and tracking support — we are not NSDL (Protean), UTIITSL, or the Income Tax Department.

Government Approval & Allotment

Final PAN number allotment, Aadhaar biometric verification, and physical card printing/dispatch rest entirely with the Income Tax Department of India and authorized NSDL / UTIITSL portals.

Transparent Assistance Fee

Our operator assistance fee covers pre-submission data audit, photo/signature cropping (DPI format), and live status tracking. Official government portal fees (₹106.90 for new PAN) are billed transparently at actuals.

Document Accuracy & Declarations

Applicants must ensure uploaded identity proofs and photo/signature are authentic and match official records. Correct details ensure rapid 2–3 day processing and avoid portal queries.

Statutory Compliance Notice: Obtaining or possessing more than one PAN card is strictly prohibited under Section 272B of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (penalty up to ₹10,000). If you possess multiple PANs, please select our Duplicate PAN Surrender service.

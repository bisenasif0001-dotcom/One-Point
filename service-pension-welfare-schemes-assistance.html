COMPLIANCE & TERMS

Important Information

Review these guidelines before submitting your application. Our process is fully aligned with official department requirements.

Independent Digital Facilitation

One Point Digital Services is a private customer facilitation desk. We assist applicants with online form filing, document verification, biometric life certificates & tracking. We are not a government department.

Official Verification & Sanction

Eligibility scrutiny, field inquiry (Gram Panchayat / Lekhpal / Tehsildar), and pension sanctioning are solely determined by the Social Welfare Department (SSPY UP) or respective authorities.

Statutory Free Portals & Assistance Charges

Filing on official portals (SSPY / Family ID / e-Shram) is ₹0 (Statutory Free). One Point charges an operator fee strictly for document pre-audit, portal entry, biometric DLC generation & follow-up.

Applicant Data & NPCI Seeding

Applicants must provide genuine documents (Aadhaar, active DBT/NPCI seeded bank passbook, income certificate). Inaccurate data or unseeded bank accounts may cause department rejection or delayed pension credit.

Approval, eligibility verification, and monthly direct benefit transfers (DBT) are governed exclusively by official Social Welfare authorities.

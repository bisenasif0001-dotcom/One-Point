DOCUMENT GUIDELINES

Aadhaar Document Guidance & Checklist

Review what documents you should carry before your visit to the authorised centre. Carry original documents only.

Mandatory

Proof of Identity (POI)

Valid photo identification confirming the applicant's full legal name:

  • Passport or PAN Card
  • Voter ID (EPIC Card)
  • Driving License
  • Ration Card with applicant photo
  • Govt issued Photo Identity Card
Must contain clear photo & name
Mandatory

Proof of Address (POA)

Official residential address proof matching the current state/local jurisdiction:

  • Electricity / Water Bill (< 3 months)
  • Bank Passbook with stamped photo
  • Voter ID or Indian Passport
  • Registered Rent Agreement
  • Property Tax Receipt / Domicile
Recent bill / stamped passbook
DOB Proof

Proof of Date of Birth

Certified date of birth record for verified age validation:

  • Birth Certificate (Municipal / CRS)
  • SSLC / 10th Class Marksheet
  • Indian Passport
  • PAN Card (for adults)
  • Govt Pension / Service Book
Exact DD/MM/YYYY matching
Children / POR

Proof of Relationship (POR)

Required for children (0–5 yrs) & minor Baal Aadhaar enrolment:

  • Child's Official Birth Certificate
  • Parent's Valid Aadhaar Card
  • Head of Family (HoF) declaration
  • Ration card showing child's name
  • Passport mentioning parents' names
Parent must accompany child
Mandatory Kendra Guidelines: Carry original hard copies of all supporting documents during your appointment visit. Authorised Aadhaar Seva Kendras scan original physical documents directly into the UIDAI portal and return them immediately. Photocopies or digital phone scans alone cannot be accepted for biometric capture.

Legal Drafting & Documentation Scope

We provide comprehensive drafting assistance tailored to individual, commercial, and court requirements.

Custom Drafting & Formatting Affidavits, lease agreements, partnership deeds, declarations, NDAs, and contracts customized to exact party conditions.
Clause Structuring & Legal Tone Standard legal definitions, indemnity terms, dispute resolution, and jurisdiction clauses formatted per Indian court norms.

Document Checklist by Drafting Category

Select the category below to view specific proofs needed for draft preparation.

Deponent Identity Proof Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License with matching name and address.
Reason / Matter of Declaration Specific purpose details (e.g. name spelling correction, gap year, lost document FIR/complaint, address change).
Address & Relationship Details Father's/Spouse's name, age, occupation, and complete current residential address.
Passport Size Photo (if applicable) Recent passport-size photo for specific court or government identity affidavits.
Both Parties KYC Proofs Aadhaar and PAN cards of Landlord/Owner (First Party) and Tenant/Buyer (Second Party).
Property Complete Address House/Flat number, floor, street, locality, landmark, city, and PIN code.
Financial Terms & Tenancy Period Monthly rent amount, security deposit, lock-in period, escalation percentage, and maintenance terms.
Witness Details (2 Persons) Names, addresses, and ID proofs of two witnesses required at the time of execution.
Partners & Entity Identity Aadhaar and PAN of all partners / company directors, proposed firm trade name.
Capital & Profit Sharing Ratio Individual capital contribution, profit/loss sharing percentage, and remuneration terms.
Confidentiality Scope (for NDA) Definition of proprietary information, duration of non-disclosure, and non-compete terms.
Registered Office Address Proof Electricity bill, municipal tax receipt, or rent agreement for principal place of business.

Fulfillment Flow

Document scanning timeline

1

Document Submission

Customer hands over physical pages, marksheets, or certificates to our operator.

2

Scanning & Quality Check

Each page is scanned using automatic document feeders (ADF) with edge-cropping rules.

3

Digital Optimization

Scan files are optimized (contrast boost, page deskew) and combined into PDF/JPEG files.

4

Secure Delivery

Files are shared directly to the customer's phone via email, WhatsApp, or secure link.

Important Passport Information

Please review these statutory guidelines and service terms before starting your application.

Physical PSK Visit Mandatory

Biometric and photograph capture require physical presence of the applicant at Lucknow PSK / POPSK on scheduled appointment date.

Original Physical Documents

Applicants must present genuine original DOB, address, and educational (Non-ECR) certificates for official counter verification.

Police Station Verification

Post-appointment police verification inquiry conducted by your local police station is required before final printing and dispatch.

Transparent Assistance Fee

Official MEA portal fee is separate at actuals. Our ₹350 operator fee covers form drafting, document audit & slot booking.

Client Responsibility

Mandatory Document Accuracy & Verification Standards

To avoid portal rejections, applicants must furnish authentic, clear, and up-to-date documentation. Correct records ensure expedited officer approval without delay.

Valid Identity & PAN Card

PAN is mandatory for all business entities. Aadhaar must be mobile-linked for instant OTP authentication & e-signing.

Registered Office Premise Proof

Electricity bill, property tax receipt, or rent agreement accompanied by Owner NOC (No Objection Certificate).

Cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy bearing the applicant's registered name for seamless GST and payout audits.

Authenticity Undertaking

The applicant is legally responsible for the genuineness and accuracy of details submitted for government filing.

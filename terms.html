01 / Scope of Assistance

Facilitation Scope & Agency Status

Boundaries of assisted digital utility services provided by One Point.

One Point Digital Services (Bisen One Point Suvidha Kendra) operates as a private utility facilitation and document assistance centre located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.

Non-Government Entity Notice: One Point is an independent private enterprise. We are not a government ministry, department, or statutory authority. We provide assisted online data entry, document formatting, scanning, and tracking services on behalf of applicants.

Our services are limited to facilitating submissions on publicly accessible government and statutory portals (e.g. NSDL, UTIITSL, UPSSSC, DigiLocker, GSTN, IRCTC) and providing retail printing and custom merchandise services.

02 / User Obligations

User Obligations & Draft Verification

Applicant responsibilities regarding authentic data submission and draft inspection.

By requesting services through our online platform, WhatsApp desk, or physical walk-in centre, you agree to:

  • Provide Authentic Documents: Furnish genuine and legally valid identity proofs, certificates, and credentials. Submitting forged, altered, or fraudulent documents is strictly illegal under the Indian Penal Code (IPC) and IT Act, 2000.
  • Review Draft Previews: Carefully inspect draft previews generated by our operators (including spellings of names, parent details, date of birth, category, and educational marks) before authorizing final submission.
  • Live OTP Authorization: Provide portal authentication OTPs promptly when required for portal login or payment authorization.
03 / Pricing & Fees

Pricing, Government Fees & Invoices

Transparent fee separation and mandatory GST tax invoice generation.

All pricing displayed on our website and counter rate cards is divided into two distinct components:

  • Mandatory Government / Portal Fees: Statutory fees levied directly by government departments or examination boards (e.g., PAN processing fees, UPSSSC exam charges, GST registration challans).
  • One Point Facilitation Charge: Fixed service fee for operator drafting, document resizing, form verification, and customer support.

An itemized GST tax invoice is generated for every completed transaction. We do not charge hidden commissions or undocumented middleman markups.

04 / Cancellation Rules

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear terms regarding fee refunds before and after portal submission.

Because government portals charge non-refundable challans immediately upon form submission, our refund terms operate as follows:

  • Cancellation Prior to Portal Submission: If you request cancellation before our operator submits your form to the official portal, 100% of your paid amount will be refunded.
  • Post-Portal Submission: Once an application is officially filed and a government acknowledgement / challan number is generated, government fees are strictly non-refundable as per departmental rules.
  • Operator Filing Error: In the rare event of a typographical error attributable solely to our operator (deviating from the applicant's verified draft sign-off), One Point will re-file the correction at zero additional facilitation cost.
05 / Physical Store

Physical Products & OneMart Orders

Terms governing PVC smart cards, framed photo prints, and merchandise.

For custom retail and store products (such as personalized photo frames, UV-printed PVC cards, and customized corporate stationery):

  • Custom Merchandise: Personalized products manufactured to custom specifications cannot be cancelled once printing has commenced.
  • Transit Damage: If a physical product arrives damaged during courier transit, notify our support desk with unboxing photos within 24 hours of delivery for a free replacement.
  • Counter Pickup: Orders scheduled for Lucknow Suvidha Kendra pickup must be collected within 30 days of readiness notification.
06 / Legal Disclaimers

Limitation of Liability & Disclaimers

Departmental approval discretion and third-party server dependencies.

While we ensure accurate draft preparation and timely submission, One Point Digital Services explicitly disclaims liability for:

  • Departmental Discretion: Rejection, delays, or inquiries issued by government authorities resulting from incomplete eligibility, document discrepancies, or departmental policy revisions.
  • Government Server Outages: Temporary downtime, slow response times, or unexpected deadline closures on official government servers.
  • Indirect Damages: Any consequential, incidental, or indirect loss arising from delayed government sanctioning or missed deadlines outside our direct operational control.

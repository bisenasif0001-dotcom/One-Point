COMPLIANCE & TERMS

Important Information

Please review these service guidelines and department terms before submitting your application.

Independent Assistance Service

One Point provides private application facilitation for form drafting, document checks, and guidance. We are not the Food & Civil Supplies Department or state government.

Official Approval & Verification

Final verification, family unit approval, fair price shop (FPS) allotment, and Ration Card issuance are handled solely by the Food & Civil Supplies Department.

Transparent Assistance Fee

Statutory FCS application fee is ₹50 (or free where applicable). Our ₹149 fee is for application assistance, document pre-audit, and tracking support.

Applicant Document Responsibility

Applicants must provide genuine and accurate Aadhaar, income, and residence documents. False declarations may result in rejection by supply officers.

Approval, eligibility verification, and physical/digital Ration Card issuance are governed exclusively by official Food & Civil Supplies authorities.

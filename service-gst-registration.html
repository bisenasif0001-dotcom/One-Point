Who Should Register for GST?

GST registration is mandatory for certain turnover thresholds and business operating models under the GST Act.

Turnover Threshold Limit Businesses with annual turnover exceeding ₹40 Lakhs (for Goods) or ₹20 Lakhs (for Services / Special Category States).
Inter-State Suppliers Any trader, manufacturer or professional supplying goods or services across state borders, regardless of annual turnover.
E-Commerce Sellers Mandatory for all merchants selling goods or services through Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Swiggy, Zomato, or own portal.
Voluntary Registration & ITC Businesses registering voluntarily to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on purchases and issue GST tax invoices to B2B clients.

01 / Pre-Filing Cancellations

Pre-Submission Cancellation & Full Refund

Conditions under which 100% of your paid amount is returned.

We believe in fair, transparent dealings. If you have made a payment for any citizen, student, or business registration service but wish to cancel before official submission work begins:

100% Full Refund Guarantee: If cancellation is requested while your request is in "Payment Received" or "Documents Uploaded" status and prior to our operator initiating filing on the target government portal, you are entitled to a full 100% refund of all charges.
  • Cancellation requests can be placed instantly through our WhatsApp Helpdesk (+91 9473946181) or via email.
  • Zero cancellation penalty or hidden processing deduction will be applied.
02 / Official Portal Fees

Government Portal Challans & Statutory Rules

Non-refundability of government fees once deposited into official treasury accounts.

Government departments (including Income Tax Department / NSDL, UPSSSC, SSC, UTIITSL, DigiLocker, and IRCTC) enforce strict statutory guidelines:

  • Treasury Non-Refundability: Once an application is officially filed and the government fee/challan has been remitted to the respective department's account, government fees cannot be recalled or refunded by One Point.
  • Application Rejections: If a government department rejects an application due to applicant ineligibility, discrepancies in official records, or forged documents, the government fee remains non-refundable as per statutory department circulars.
03 / Duplicate UPI Payments

Duplicate UPI Payments & Erroneous Debits

Expedited turnaround for duplicate scans, network timeouts, or double debits.

In cases where network latency or multiple QR code scans result in more than one debit for a single order:

  • 24–48 Hour Verification: Report the duplicate transaction with your 12-digit UPI UTR number and payment screenshot. Our billing desk cross-checks with payment gateway logs.
  • Automatic Reversal: Once confirmed, the duplicate amount is queued for immediate automatic reversal to your originating UPI ID / bank account.
04 / Operator Error Guarantee

Operator Error Protection & Free Correction

Assurances against errors made during data entry by our Suvidha Kendra team.

We take immense pride in our attention to detail and accuracy. In the rare scenario where an error occurs:

  • Free Correction Filing: If an error is made by our operator that contradicts the applicant's verified draft sign-off, One Point will pay all applicable re-filing and portal modification fees at zero additional cost to the customer.
  • Full Refund Alternative: If re-filing cannot remedy the issue, a 100% full refund of both facilitation and portal fees will be issued.
05 / Physical Store Products

Physical Merchandise, PVC Cards & Photo Frames

Cancellation and transit replacement policy for customized retail goods.

For custom retail and store products (such as personalized photo frames, UV-printed PVC cards, and customized corporate stationery):

  • Customized Items: Once custom printing or laser engraving has begun, retail orders cannot be cancelled.
  • Transit Damage Guarantee: If your framed print or PVC card arrives damaged, cracked, or misprinted, share clear photos within 24 hours of delivery. We will ship a brand-new replacement at zero charge.
06 / Settlement Timelines

Settlement Mode & Refund Timelines

Standard bank crediting cycles for approved reversals.

All approved refunds are settled transparently through banking channels:

  • Original Payment Method: Refunds are credited strictly back to the source account (UPI ID, Debit/Credit Card, or Net Banking) used during checkout.
  • Processing Timelines:
    • UPI Reversals: 24 to 48 business hours.
    • Debit / Credit Cards: 3 to 5 banking days (dependent on card issuing bank).

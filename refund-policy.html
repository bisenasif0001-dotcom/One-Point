Refund Policy

Payment review and refund rules.

Last updated: May 14, 2026. Refunds depend on service stage, payment verification and third-party official fee rules.

Raise Payment Issue Track Request

Before Processing

If payment is made but processing has not started, you may request cancellation and refund review through WhatsApp or the contact form.

After Processing Starts

Service assistance charges may be non-refundable once document review, form filling, booking support or official submission work has started.

Official Fees

Government portal fees, gateway charges, ticket charges and other third-party fees follow the refund rules of the relevant department or provider.

Duplicate Payments

Duplicate or wrong UPI payments should be reported with transaction ID and payment proof. Approved refunds are processed after verification.

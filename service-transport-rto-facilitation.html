COMPLIANCE & GUIDELINES

Important Information & Guidelines

Review key rules regarding eligibility, documents, government statutory fees, and RTO visits.

Eligibility Requirements for RTO Services

Learner's Licence (LL) Age 18+ / 16+

Min. 18 years for MCWG (Motorcycle with Gear) & LMV (Cars); 16 years for gearless 2-wheelers (<50cc) with parent/guardian consent.

Permanent Licence (DL) After 30 Days

Valid active Learner's Licence held for at least 30 days (valid up to 180 days from the date of issue) before track test.

Commercial & Transport DL Age 20+

Minimum 20 years of age with at least 1 year of continuous valid LMV driving experience and medical fitness Form 1A.

Vehicle RC & Transfer Registered Owner

Registered owner or authorized legal buyer/transferee with valid sale deed, NOC, Form 29/30, and active insurance.

Standard Document Checklist

Proof of Identity (POI) Mandatory

Aadhaar Card (with active linked mobile for e-KYC), PAN Card, Voter ID (EPIC), or Indian Passport.

Proof of Address (POA) Mandatory

Aadhaar Card, Electricity/Water Utility Bill, Ration Card, or Registered Rent Agreement with police verification.

Proof of Age & DOB Mandatory

10th Class School Marksheet/Certificate, Municipal Birth Certificate, or valid Indian Passport.

Medical Fitness Forms Form 1 / 1A

Form 1 (Self-declaration) for under 40 years; Form 1A (Registered Doctor Certificate) for 40+ years or commercial classes.

Government Fee Structure

Official Statutory Fees Direct Parivahan

Learner's Licence (₹150 + ₹50 test fee per class), Permanent DL (₹200 + ₹300 smart card/test fee) deposited directly.

RTO Appointment & Track Test Guidelines

Online LL Safety Test Take From Home

In UP Sarathi, applicants with Aadhaar authentication can take the online computer test from home without visiting RTO.

Permanent DL Driving Test Mandatory Track

Physical driving skill test on automated RTO test tracks (LDA Colony / Transport Nagar) is required for final DL.

Slot Booking & Coordination Earliest Available

We track open calendar slots to lock in the fastest test appointment fitting your schedule.

FAQs

Common Transport & RTO Questions.

Everything you need to know about our Transport & RTO Facilitation assistance answered clearly.

Any Indian resident aged 18+ is eligible for Motorcycle with Gear (MCWG) and Light Motor Vehicle (LMV/Car). Applicants aged 16–18 can apply for gearless 2-wheelers (<50cc) with parent/guardian consent.

A Learner's Licence is valid for 6 months (180 days) across India. You can apply for a Permanent Driving Licence 30 days after the date of issue of your Learner's Licence.

Standard documents include: 1. Aadhaar Card (with linked mobile number), 2. Proof of Age (10th marksheet or birth certificate), 3. Form 1 Self-Declaration (or Form 1A for 40+ years/commercial), and 4. Passport photo and signature scan.

For a **Learner's Licence** in UP, the test can be completed online from home via Aadhaar e-KYC. For a **Permanent Driving Licence**, a physical visit to the RTO for the vehicle driving track test and biometrics is mandatory.

Learner's Licence preparation and online test processing take 1–3 working days. Permanent DL slot booking and smart card dispatch take approximately 7–15 working days depending on RTO testing slots.

The official statutory fee paid to the Transport Department is ₹350 for Learner's Licence. One Point charges an operator assistance fee of ₹149 for document audit, portal drafting, test guidance, and status tracking (Total ₹499 package).

Compliance & Terms

Important Information

Please review these travel booking guidelines carefully before you confirm. Our desk operates in line with official operator and portal rules.

Independent Desk

One Point provides assisted travel booking support. We are a private travel desk — not IRCTC, any airline, bus operator or government body.

01

Booking Confirmation

Final ticket confirmation, seat or berth availability and fares depend entirely on the official IRCTC, airline and operator systems at the time of booking.

02

Assistance Fee

The service fee covers operator booking assistance only. Actual ticket and package fares are charged separately as per live rates at booking time.

03

Passenger Details

Customer must provide correct passenger names, ID, age and travel dates. Incorrect details can cause boarding denial and remain the traveller's responsibility.

04

Tickets & Updates

Tickets, PNR and booking confirmations are shared on WhatsApp and Email after successful payment confirmation and operator processing.

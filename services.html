E-Services

Govt ID & Certificates checklist

Aadhaar Card of Applicant

Mandatory for PAN, Ayushman, Domicile, Caste, Voter, and Passport services.

Address Proof & Registry

Electricity bill, Rent agreement, or water bill required for Passport and certificates.

Applicant Photo & Signature Scan

Keep a clear passport-size photo and signature ready for online upload.

Aadhaar-Linked Mobile Number

Essential for receiving verification OTPs during official portal submissions.

Supporting Family Proofs

Ration card (for Ayushman PMJAY) or educational records (for Voter ID correction).

Student Form checklist

Previous Marksheets & Roll IDs

Keep board or university roll numbers handy for admission and exam forms.

Aadhaar Card of the Student

Necessary for applying to scholarship benefits and college enrollment portals.

Student Photo & Signature Scan

Correct size and aspect ratio scans are required for error-free form uploads.

Income & Caste Certificates

Required for verifying eligibility and claiming scholarship benefits.

ProServe

Business filing & Licensing checklist

PAN Card of Proprietor or Firm

Required to start GST, MSME, DSC, and corporate registration filings.

Aadhaar of Owner / Directors

Linked mobile number is necessary for online verification and OTP signature check.

Business Address Proof

Electricity bill, rent agreement, or NOC from the property owner is mandatory.

Bank Details or Cancelled Cheque

Proof of active bank account required to update GST portal database.

Firm Name & Nature of Activity

Describe your trade name and services for accurate licensing and category setup.

CSC & Utility

Govt Scheme & Utility checklist

Aadhaar Card of Beneficiary

Required for PM Kisan, Ayushman, pension registration, and all CSC scheme enrollments.

Bank Passbook or Account Details

Active bank account with IFSC code needed for PM Kisan and pension benefit transfers.

Land Records / Kisan ID (PM Kisan)

Khasra-Khatauni or Kisan registration number for PM Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme.

Aadhaar-Linked Mobile Number

OTP verification is required on CSC portal for insurance and utility scheme enrollments.

Existing Policy or Scheme Reference

Previous policy number or enrollment ID needed for insurance renewal or complaint tracking.

Design Brief & Logos

Share a rough layout, color preferences, and text details for Banner, Card, or Flex design.

Eligibility Criteria

Check your eligibility before submitting your assistance request.

First-Time Voters (Age 18+): Indian citizens who are 18 years of age or will turn 18 on the upcoming qualifying quarter date.
Shifted / Relocated Citizens: Voters who have moved to a new residential address within the same assembly constituency or across states.
Correction Applicants: Existing voter card holders with spelling mistakes in Name, Father/Husband Name, Date of Birth, or Photo.
Duplicate Card Seekers: Citizens whose original physical Voter ID is lost, stolen, damaged, or mutilated.
Applicants must be ordinary residents of India and possess valid address and age proofs.

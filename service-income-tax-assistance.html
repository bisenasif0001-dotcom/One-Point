Required Document Checklist by Taxpayer Type

Select your taxpayer category to view the exact document checklist:

PAN Card & Aadhaar Card Primary taxpayer identification; ensure mobile number is linked to Aadhaar for OTP verification.
Form 16 from Employer Part A (TDS summary) and Part B (salary breakdown and chapter VI-A deductions).
Bank Account Details Bank statements, IFSC code, and pre-validated account details for direct refund credit.
Tax Saving Proofs (80C / 80D) LIC premium receipts, PPF passbook, health insurance receipts, tuition fees, and home loan interest.
Books of Accounts / Sales Summary Gross turnover, receipts, sales ledgers, or presumptive income figures (Section 44AD / 44ADA).
GST Returns (GSTR-3B / 1) GST turnover reconciliation with income tax portal reporting.

Bank Current & Savings Statements Annual bank statements showing business deposits and expense payments.
10-Digit PAN Number Active PAN registered on the e-Filing portal.
Portal Login Password or Aadhaar OTP Password to access official IT portal or Aadhaar OTP for instant password reset.
Assessment Year Details Relevant financial year for which Form 26AS, AIS, or refund dispatch is to be tracked.

Who Should File Income Tax Returns?

Filing ITR maintains clean financial proof for loan approvals, VISA processing, and tax refunds.

Salaried Individuals & Pensioners Anyone with total annual gross income exceeding the basic exemption limit (₹2.5L Old / ₹3L New).
Business Owners & Professionals Consultants, doctors, lawyers, shopkeepers, and freelancers with gross receipts.
Claiming TDS / Income Tax Refunds Anyone whose tax was deducted at source by employers, banks, or clients and wants a refund.
High Financial Transactions & VISA Deposited >₹50L in savings/current account, foreign travel >₹2L, or applying for bank loans & visas.

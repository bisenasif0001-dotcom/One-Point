01 / Data Collection

Information We Collect

Scope of personal and application inputs collected during service fulfillment.

When you initiate a service request online or walk in to our Lucknow Suvidha Kendra, we collect only the information necessary to prepare, verify, and complete your application:

  • Contact Data: Full name, mobile / WhatsApp number, email address, and physical delivery address (for physical deliverables such as laminated PVC cards or framed photo prints).
  • Application-Specific Inputs: Registration numbers, date of birth, educational marksheets, parent names, and category certificates requested by destination portals.
  • Transaction Records: Payment gateway transaction reference numbers, billing timestamps, and invoice metadata for tax accounting.
02 / Identity Document Handling

Identity Document Handling

Procedures for safeguarding Aadhaar, PAN, and government identification credentials.

National identity documents are processed strictly in alignment with Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable department guidelines:

Identity Safety Protocol: Where full identity numbers are not mandated by the target portal, Masked Aadhaar is preferred. Live OTP verification is conducted only with your direct authorization.
  • Physical identity cards scanned at our counter are returned to you immediately after digitization.
  • Digital scans received via WhatsApp or portal are saved in isolated session directories accessible only to the assigned filing operator.
  • Citizens may request optional digital watermarking (*"For One Point Filing Only"*) on submitted scans.
03 / Purpose & Processing

How We Use Your Data

Specific operational purposes for which information is processed.

Your data is purpose-bound and utilized exclusively for:

  • Filing government application forms and portal submissions on your behalf.
  • Document quality enhancement (cropping, photo resizing to board specifications).
  • Live milestone tracking and WhatsApp status dispatch.
  • Issuing official GST tax invoices and accounting records.

No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or contact details to third-party advertisers, lead brokers, or marketing networks.

04 / Payments & Invoices

Payment Security & Invoicing

PCI-DSS compliant payment processing and statutory invoice records.

Digital payments on One Point are processed through RBI-authorized payment gateways (Razorpay, verified UPI QR). All payment communications use HTTPS TLS 1.3 encryption.

  • No Raw Card Storage: One Point does not store your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or net banking passwords. Card tokenization and authentication are handled entirely by the certified payment gateway.
  • Tax Invoices: Official GST tax invoices are generated for every transaction and retained for statutory audits as required under Indian taxation laws.
05 / Third-Party Processors

Approved Service Processors

Infrastructure and gateway partners supporting service delivery.

To execute digital operations, certain data is processed through verified third-party technical providers:

  • Destination Government Portals: NSDL, UTIITSL, UPSSSC, SSC, DigiLocker, IRCTC (data submitted per citizen application).
  • Payment Gateways: Razorpay / UPI rails for encrypted transaction processing.
  • Communication Rails: WhatsApp Business API / SMS gateways for status notifications.
  • Hosting & Storage: Secure cloud infrastructure adhering to standard data encryption at rest.
06 / Retention & Purge

Data Retention & Scheduled Purge

Clear criteria defining how long files remain in active storage.

We maintain a minimized lifecycle for citizen documents:

  • Application Files: Retained in active operator storage only during the processing, verification, and deliverable delivery period.
  • Scheduled Purging: Document scans are scheduled for secure archival or purge following delivery confirmation, unless earlier deletion is requested by the citizen.
  • Statutory Invoices: Invoice metadata (Name, Service Name, Amount, Date) is retained solely for legal and tax accounting requirements.
07 / Cookies & Analytics

Cookies & Technical Storage

Minimal essential browser storage for website functionality.

Our website utilizes minimal, privacy-respecting technical storage:

  • Session & Cart State: LocalStorage is used to maintain your cart items, active application drafts, and tracking queries across page visits.
  • No Invasive Ad Tracking: We do not run third-party tracking pixels or cross-site behavioral advertising cookies.
08 / Citizen Rights & Controls

Your Rights & Data Controls

Actionable rights you can exercise regarding your personal data.

As a citizen utilizing One Point Digital Services, you have the full right to:

  • Inspect: Request a summary of all document scans and application records on file for your Request ID.
  • Correct: Request instant correction of misspelled names, dates, or address records prior to portal submission.
  • Erase: Request on-demand deletion of all digitized identity documents upon order completion.

01 / Agency Status

Non-Government Entity & Facilitation Scope

Clear clarification of our private enterprise operational boundaries.

One Point Digital Services (Bisen One Point Suvidha Kendra) is an independent, privately owned customer service and utility facilitation enterprise based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.

Non-Affiliation Disclosure: We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operating as an official arm of any Central or State Government Ministry, Department, or Statutory Board. All trademarks, logos, and portal names (e.g. NSDL, UTIITSL, UIDAI, UPSSSC, SSC, DigiLocker, IRCTC) belong exclusively to their respective government entities.

Our role is strictly limited to assisting citizens with data entry, document digitization, photograph formatting, fee routing, and milestone tracking.

02 / Sovereign Discretion

Government Discretion & No Approval Guarantee

Statutory approval limitations and processing timelines.

While we ensure accurate preparation and timely portal submission:

  • No Guaranteed Issuance: The grant, sanction, issuance, or approval of any government certificate, PAN card, Ayushman card, voter registration, or competitive exam admit card is solely at the discretion of the issuing government authority.
  • Processing Timelines: Estimated processing days provided on our platform reflect typical historical turnaround times. Actual dispatch depends entirely on departmental verifications and official server load.
  • Server Downtime: We are not liable for delays resulting from official government server maintenance, gateway timeouts, or sudden portal deadline closures.
03 / Pricing & Updates

Accuracy of Information & Portal Fee Revisions

Policy regarding dynamic government challan revisions and notification updates.

We make every effort to maintain accurate fee schedules, eligibility criteria, and examination dates. However:

  • Government departments and recruitment boards may revise statutory fee structures, eligibility guidelines, or document rules without prior notice.
  • In case of a statutory fee hike by an official board before final filing, the applicant will be informed to settle the exact difference prior to submission.
04 / Applicant Liability

Customer Obligations & Forged Document Liability

Legal responsibility regarding genuine credentials and draft inspection.

Applicants bear full and exclusive legal responsibility for the validity of submitted credentials:

  • Prohibition of Forgery: Submitting fake, altered, or forged documents is a punishable criminal offense under the Indian Penal Code (IPC) and the Information Technology Act, 2000. One Point immediately terminates assistance for any fraudulent requests.
  • Draft Review Obligation: Applicants must thoroughly check draft previews before authorizing final payment and submission. One Point is not responsible for errors present in applicant-approved drafts.
05 / Travel & IRCTC

Travel Booking, IRCTC & Third-Party Services

Disclaimers governing railway ticketing, airline bookings, and bus reservations.

For assisted travel reservations (including train and flight bookings):

  • No Guaranteed Seat Confirmations: Waitlisted train tickets and Tatkal availability depend entirely on live IRCTC inventory at the time of booking.
  • Cancellation & Refund Rules: Train cancellation charges, clerkage deductions, and airline cancellation fees are strictly regulated by IRCTC and respective airlines.
06 / External Links

External Government Links & Third-Party Sites

Guidance on hyperlinks pointing to official state and central portals.

Our platform may provide direct hyperlinks to official government websites (e.g. incometax.gov.in, ssc.gov.in, upsssc.gov.in) solely for customer convenience and status tracking.

One Point does not own, manage, or endorse third-party web content, server performance, or privacy practices on those external portals.

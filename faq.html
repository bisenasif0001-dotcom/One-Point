General & Overview

4 Verified Answers
Q1 What is One Point Digital Services?

One Point Digital Services is a local-first, hybrid service center in Lucknow. We help everyday families, students, and businesses apply for government forms, PAN cards, certificates, GST filing, travel tickets, printing, and custom design — with human assistance at every step.

Q2 What are your working hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM at our Suvidha Kendra in LDA Colony, Lucknow. Online applications and WhatsApp assistance are monitored throughout the day.

Q3 Can I get help without visiting the center physically?

Yes! Most citizen services, form fillings, and document printings can be completely managed online. Simply select your service, share documents securely via WhatsApp or portal, pay via UPI, and receive your acknowledgement slip and final certificates digitally.

Q4 How do I track my application status?

Every request submitted through One Point receives a unique Tracking ID (e.g. OPDS-20260722-000001). You can check real-time milestone progress anytime on our Track Application page or by asking us on WhatsApp.

PAN & Government IDs

3 Verified Answers
Q1 What documents are required for a New PAN Card?

For an individual new PAN card, you only need:

  • Aadhaar Card (front and back)
  • Passport-size photograph with white background
  • Signature on plain white paper
Q2 How long does it take to get a PAN Card?

An e-PAN (digital PDF) is typically generated within 3 to 5 business days and sent to your email and WhatsApp. The physical laminated PVC card is delivered by India Post to your address within 10 to 15 days.

Q3 Can I correct my name, father's name, or DOB on an existing PAN?

Yes. We process PAN corrections and reprints. You will need your existing PAN number, Aadhaar card with corrected details, and supporting proof (e.g. 10th marksheet or gazette notification if name changed after marriage).

GST & Business Services

3 Verified Answers
Q1 What is needed for a new GST Registration?

For proprietorship GST registration, you need: PAN card, Aadhaar card, electricity bill of business address, rent agreement or NOC, passport photo, and bank account statement/cancelled cheque.

Q2 Do you assist with monthly GST Return Filing (GSTR-1 / 3B)?

Yes. We offer regular monthly and quarterly GST return filing packages for local traders and small business owners in Lucknow.

Q3 Do you support MSME / Udyam registration?

Yes. Udyam MSME certificate registration is processed same-day with immediate government certificate issuance.

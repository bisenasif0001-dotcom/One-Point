What is One Point Digital Services?

One Point Digital Services (One Point Suvidha Kendra) is a local digital service center in Lucknow that helps you with government services (PAN, Ayushman, certificates), student services (exam forms, scholarships), business registrations (GST, MSME), travel bookings, design services and a local products store — all in one place.

What are your working hours?

We are open Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. You can also WhatsApp us anytime and we will respond during business hours.

Can I get help without visiting the center?

Yes! Most services can be started online. WhatsApp us your documents and request details, and we will guide you through the entire process remotely. If a service requires physical verification, our team will confirm the visit requirement first.

How do I track my application status?

Every application gets a unique tracking ID. You can check your status on our Track Application page or WhatsApp us your tracking ID directly for an instant update.

Payment & Fees

4 questions
What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), cash at our center and bank transfer. For online requests, UPI is preferred — you will receive our UPI QR code and once payment is done, share the transaction screenshot with us for verification.

Do I pay before or after the service?

We show you the price upfront before you pay. For most services, you pay after document verification and before we begin processing. For some services like PAN Card, you will see the full fee breakdown on the service page before submitting.

Are government portal fees separate from your service charge?

Yes. Our service charge covers the assistance, form filling, document review and application submission. Any government portal fees (like passport fee, GST portal fee) are charged separately and we always inform you about both before you pay.

What is your refund policy?

If your application is rejected due to our error, we re-process at no extra charge. For government-rejected applications (due to wrong documents submitted by the customer), a re-application service charge applies. See our Refund Policy for full details.

PAN Card

3 questions
How long does a new PAN Card take?

A new PAN Card typically takes 2–3 working days after successful application. The physical card may take 7–15 days to arrive by post. An e-PAN (soft copy) is available digitally in 2–3 days.

What documents are needed for a PAN Card?

For a new PAN Card: Aadhaar Card (mandatory), Date of Birth proof (Class 10 certificate or birth certificate), and a passport-size photo. For correction: existing PAN details + proof of the change needed.

What is the fee for PAN Card service?

New PAN Card: ₹149  |  PAN Card Correction: ₹129. This includes our service charge and government fee. No hidden charges.

Business Services

3 questions
How long does GST registration take?

GST registration typically takes 3–7 working days after successful document submission on the GST portal. We handle the complete application process and keep you updated on every step.

Is MSME (Udyam) registration free?

Yes, Udyam registration on the government portal is free of charge. Our service charge of ₹299 covers the application process, form filling and certificate download assistance on your behalf.

Do I need to be present for business registrations?

Most business registrations (GST, MSME, Shop License) can be done remotely — just WhatsApp your documents. For Digital Signature (DSC), OTP and biometric verification may be required in person.

Travel Booking

3 questions
How does travel booking work?

Share your route, travel date, number of passengers and timing on WhatsApp. We check availability, share fare options, and once you confirm, we book and send the ticket on WhatsApp as PDF.

Do you book Tatkal train tickets?

Yes, we support Tatkal and Premium Tatkal booking with IRCTC. Tatkal opens at 10 AM (AC) and 11 AM (Sleeper). WhatsApp us a night before with your journey details for best results.

What is your travel service charge?

Travel service charges vary: Bus booking ₹30, Train booking ₹40, Flight booking ₹100–150, Hotel booking ₹100–200 (based on hotel rate). Tour packages are custom quoted. Government fare is charged separately.

File Quality Guide

Better files, cleaner output.

Before sending your document, follow these small guidelines so your prints stay sharp, scans remain legible, and copies look professional.

Start Print Request Check My File

Best for Printing

Use PDF Format

Keeps fonts, margins, and page layouts perfectly stable across devices.

300+ DPI Resolution

Ensures text, stamps, and photos print sharp with zero pixelation.

Correct 5mm Margins

Keep at least 5mm margin on all sides to avoid cut-off edges.

Avoid Screenshots

Low-res mobile screenshots often appear blurry or compressed in print.

Before Sending

Specify Color Mode

Clearly mention if you need Black & White or vibrant Color prints.

Check Page Sequence

Confirm multi-page documents follow correct chronological order.

State Copy Quantity

Mention the exact number of copies needed for each document.

Mention Finishing Needs

Specify if you require stapling, lamination, or spiral binding.
